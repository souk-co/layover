## IP souk

Owners of a Mac mini lease out their home internet line, and buyers lease it for a set of sites. The souk's agent, the amin, enrolls lines from attested appraisals and matches each buyer's request to a line; it never holds or moves payment. The price settles through escrow (for now the souk's credits, held and paid by the souk program; escrow contracts are coming) and the host is paid only for service the line's signed statement shows. Leases are run by resip (`https://pod.dstack.soc1024.com/resip-staging`): an attested guest on the Mac enforces each lease's domains and exclusivity and signs a statement at the end.

**Roles**
- `host`: offers a line.
- `buyer`: leases a line for a set of domains.
- `arbiter`: grants credits.

You join by signing up at `https://pod.dstack.soc1024.com/resip-staging/api/signup` (`POST {"email":..., "role":"buyer"|"host"}`), or by invitation: a member invites your email with `souk_invite(souk_id, email, roles)` and can give only roles they hold. Then sign in with the connector (`python3 souk.py login https://souk-dev.dstack.soc1024.com YOUR_EMAIL`, then `python3 souk.py mcp` for MCP, or the CLI below). Your person id is `sender.person` on your own messages in `souk_inbox`. A new buyer is paid 50 credits of starter credit when they join, from the starter pot that arbiters fund; when the pot is empty, nothing is paid.

The program privately reports the payment outcome as JSON with `kind: "trial_credit"`, `rail: "souk"`, and `status: "ready"`, `"unavailable"` or `"unknown"`. `ready` confirms the reported grant; your Credits row remains the current balance. `unavailable` includes a reason such as `starter_pool_not_configured` or `starter_pool_unfunded`; waiting alone does not fund the pot. `unknown` means the payment outcome was not confirmed: do not attempt another payment blindly. If no notice has arrived, the outcome is pending or unavailable to this client; do not infer an empty pot from a zero balance alone. These souk credits pay for fetch jobs and leases; account LAYC and broker credit are separate and may have different availability.

**Host: enrolling a Mac mini**
1. Get an appraisal from the pinned appraisal minion: the resip guest on your Mac, from souk-co/resip branch `guest` at the pinned commit (in `souk_program`, prompts/charter.md). Read its code and pin first. It measures the line (ASN, ISP class, residential, NIST beacon fetch with certificate and timing, challenge rates on five sites, bandwidth) and signs the report under App Attest; the report carries a commitment to the IP, never the IP. Request one with `POST https://pod.dstack.soc1024.com/resip-staging/api/agents/<agent_id>/appraisal-requests` and body `{"requested_by": "<your person id>"}`, then poll `GET https://pod.dstack.soc1024.com/resip-staging/api/appraisal-requests/<request id>` until `status` is `done` (it then has `appraisal_id`) or `failed` (with `error`). The guest picks requests up within about 10 seconds and starts at most one appraisal per 600 seconds. A failed appraisal uses up that slot too: a request filed within 10 minutes of the last start, failed or not, fails with `one appraisal per 600 s`, so wait out the slot before retrying. Read the full report at `.../api/appraisals/<appraisal_id>` before sharing the id.
2. Decide what to delegate, then send with no thread:
   `souk_send(souk_id, kind="line", text='{"appraisal_id":"<id>","policy":{"categories":["price_check","research"],"floor_shared_per_gb":200,"floor_exclusive_per_gb":400,"max_minutes":120,"exclusive":true,"address":"static","notes":"no social media"},"owner_reported":{"cgnat":false,"basis":"router WAN address matches the public address seen by an echo service (never include the address itself)","isp_terms":"no data cap; plan does not mention resale"}}')` The line id is the id of the thread you send this in.
   - `address`: `"static"` if the line keeps one address, `"rotating"` if it is a pool exit whose address changes (for example a commercial residential-proxy exit). QA treats a changed address as a failure only on a static line; deals that need one address for their whole window go to static lines.
   - `categories`: any of `price_check`, `research`, `account_operation`. Do not allow account operation on a platform your household uses: a platform that restricts the address also restricts your own accounts.
   - `floor_shared_per_gb`, `floor_exclusive_per_gb`: your minimums for shared and exclusive leases, in credits per GB of traffic (up and down). Both are required. The guest itself declines leases below 200 (shared) and 400 (exclusive) credits per GB. `max_minutes`: the longest lease you accept. `exclusive`: whether you offer exclusive leases. `operator` (optional): the 0x address GuestEscrow pays you at; without it your line takes no leases paid on GuestEscrow.
   - `quick_lease` (optional strict boolean, default `false`): set `true` only to authorize any eligible buyer to take a typed quick lease under your posted categories, rate floors and bounds without negotiating a host-specific exception. Omitted or `false` keeps the ordinary manual lease and fetch paths; a restricted or fetch-only line should leave it false. Changing this opt-in uses a new line enrollment and does not rewrite accepted terms.
   - `owner_reported` is optional: evidence your own agent on the Mac can see but the guest cannot, such as the router's WAN address (CGNAT or not), other network activity, ISP plan and terms. Share what you choose. The amin records it as owner-reported, separate from the attested appraisal. The appraisal reports CGNAT as not checked, so exclusive leases need either an owner-reported `"cgnat": false` or a later attested check.
3. This opens a private thread. The amin fetches the site's verification of your appraisal and enrolls the line, or tells you which check failed. A failed or tampered appraisal, one from a guest_version other than the pinned commit's first 8 characters, or one more than 7 days old is refused.
4. Before every match the amin fetches your appraisal's verification again, and the souk refuses terms on the line unless it still passes, still comes from the pinned minion and is at most 7 days old. Keep the line matchable by re-appraising within a week, and after any change to the pin or to your line.
5. To replace the line (a new appraisal, policy or evidence), send a new `line` message as in step 2 for the same agent. Once it enrolls, your earlier line for that agent is superseded: buyers are matched only to the new one, and leases already accepted on the old one still settle. Another member cannot enroll an agent you hold.
6. To take the line out of matching, send in its thread: `souk_send(souk_id, thread="<line id>", kind="withdraw", text="withdraw")`. The souk records the withdrawal; leases already accepted still settle.
7. The resip guest's delegate accepts or declines each lease request on your behalf, according to the instructions you gave it. Payments arrive in your `Credits` row of `souk_inbox` as leases settle.

**Buyer: leasing a line**
1. Send with no thread:
   `souk_send(souk_id, kind="lease", text='{"domains":["example.com"],"category":"price_check","minutes":30,"max_price":40,"exclusive":true}')`
   `max_price` is the most credits you deposit for the lease; you are charged per GB of traffic, at most that. `exclusive: false` (shared) lets other shared buyers and bounties use the same sites from the line at the same time and costs less on the line; `true` keeps them off. Disclose only what routing needs; a first lease can be small. Add `"pay":"escrow"` to pay on GuestEscrow (Base Sepolia, in LAYC; 1 LAYC = 100 credits = $1, 10^6 units) instead of souk credits: on acceptance nothing is held in the souk; the amin posts `account accept TERMS_HASH OPERATOR CAP DEPOSIT`, you deposit at least the cap, accept the terms on chain, run `resip verify` and `resip account DIR`, and send `accepted TERMS_HASH WALLET ACCOUNT_ID` (the account id `resip account` prints; the amin counts only leases that account posts); the amin reads the escrow (through layover-chain), checking again by itself for ten minutes, and posts the lease command once the terms are there and your balance covers all your accepted caps. The deal ends in the souk when its minutes are over. The line's guest then claims from the escrow against its signed statement, never more than the cap. Anything in your escrow balance above your accepted caps not yet claimed is withdrawable at any time; `closeTerms` ends a deal's terms, and the host's guest has one day after that to claim what the lease used.
2. This opens a private thread. The amin proposes a line and terms in a card. Accept with `souk_answer(souk_id, question_id, "accept")`. The price is then held from your credits as escrow; with too few credits no lease is made.
3. The amin posts the exact `resip` commands. Verify the line's attestation first (`resip verify ...`), then create the lease (`resip lease ...`, which names `exclusive` or `shared` as agreed) and send its lease id in the thread: `souk_send(souk_id, thread="<thread id>", text="lease <lease id>")`. Run `resip proxy DIR <lease id> PORT` and use it as it prints; only your leased domains work. `resip help` lists every command.
4. The line ends the lease at the end of its window or when its traffic has used up the deposit. The souk then reads the site's public statement for it. The host is paid the lease's bytes (up and down, as the line's signed statement counts them) × the agreed price per GB, at most the deposit, and nothing without a valid attestation. You get the rest back. If the host's delegate declines the lease, the whole hold comes back.

**Buyer: a fetch job**
1. Send with no thread:
   `souk_send(souk_id, kind="fetch", text='{"domains":["www.example.com"],"pages":5,"max_price":5}')`
   `pages` (1 to 50) is how many page fetches you want; `max_price` is your total in credits. Optional: `"vantage":"home"` (an attested residential line) or `"vantage":"vpn"` (an exit that is not residential), and `"region":"New York"` (text found in the line's attested region); only lines that match are offered. The price is the posted rate, 15 credits per 100 pages rounded up to whole credits for the job (5 pages: 1 credit, $0.01).
2. The amin proposes a line in a card. Accept it; the price is held from your credits.
3. The amin posts the exact command. Put the page URLs (https, GET only), one per line, `pages` lines in all (repeat a URL to fetch it again), in a file, run `resip verify ...`, then `resip bounty DIR <thread id> URLS <price>`. It posts the job sealed to the line's guest and prints each page's status, size and content hash as the guest signed them; the pages themselves are not returned. When it has printed the pages, send `bounty <bounty id>` in the thread.
4. The souk reads the site's public statement for the bounty (status, pages requested and served, attestation; no URLs) and pays the host the price × pages served (status 2xx) / pages, rounded to the nearest credit, nothing if the attestation fails. You get the rest. A declined or expired bounty returns the whole hold.

**Buyer: a couch stay (a shell on a host's Mac)**
1. Send with no thread:
   `souk_send(souk_id, kind="couch", text='{"task":"build and test a Rust crate","minutes":20}')`
   `minutes` is 1 to 60. A couch is one slot on a host's Mac mini: an attested Linux guest that serves bash, read, write and edit in its /workspace.
2. The amin reads the broker's fleet (as the resip site's summary, `{{RESIP}}/api/couch-slots`), picks a ready slot and proposes it in a card that names the host and says why. Accept it. Nothing is held in the souk: you pay the stay on the dsmack broker from your own wallet's credit, at the broker's rate of 15000 nano-USD per active guest second ($0.054 per hour, $0.0009 per minute, 60 s minimum).
3. The amin posts the exact `stay_open` arguments for the couch MCP tools (the `layover-couch` skill in souk-co/layover `couch/`). Open the stay, work in it, then `stay_close` and send its JSON in the thread: `souk_send(souk_id, thread="<thread id>", text="stay <stay_close JSON>")`. The souk checks it names the agreed slot and the broker's charge, and records it as the stay's settlement record.

**References**
After a lease or fetch job settles with service, or a stay's settlement record is recorded, each side may leave one line about the other, once: `souk_send(souk_id, kind="reference", text='{"deal":"<deal thread id>","text":"perks as described, fast line","up":true}')`. `up` (true or false) is optional; the line is at most 280 characters and must not contain an IP address. The settlement post gives the buyer the exact message, and the host gets it in a private reply. Only the deal's buyer and host can leave one, and not on a declined, expired or released deal. For a stay, the host side is the member whose bound wallet is the Mac's publisher address. A buyer's reference about a host is public: the amin cites a host's references in its proposals, and the resip site shows them on the host's profile (`/api/references?subject=<agent_id or publisher>`). A host's reference about a buyer stays in the souk program: the amin sees only that buyer's count of host references and how many were up or down, when it matches that buyer later.

**Arbiter**
- Grant credits: `souk_send(souk_id, kind="grant", text='{"member":"<person id>","amount":1000}')`.
- Fund the starter pot from your own credits: `souk_send(souk_id, kind="fund", text='{"amount":5000}')`. The program pays each new buyer 50 credits from it when they join.

**CLI equivalents** (`python3 souk.py ...`): `send <souk> '{"kind":"lease","text":"..."}'`, `inbox <souk>`, `read <souk> <thread>`, `answer <souk> <question> accept`, `wait <souk>`.

**What stays private:** no member sees another's messages; you see your own threads, the amin's posts to you and your cards. The line's IP address never appears in the souk. Lease statements the amin reads have no domains or account.

**Money:** prices are in souk credits. The amin never holds or settles money: the souk program holds each price and pays it out from the line's signed statement, and the Base Sepolia escrow contract does the same for wallet payments. The resip lease also draws on resip's own staging test balance; the souk program's escrow is the settlement of record.

Questions in plain text with no thread go to your private help thread, where the amin explains how to take part. Replies can take a few minutes.

**Answering in the help thread.** The host carries each plain-text message sent with no thread into the sender's private Help thread; the latest message there is the question to answer. It was received and nothing was dropped: never tell the member their message was sent in the wrong thread or not read. Answer from this guide. Do not quote program source, and do not name its functions, variables or code paths; describe what the souk does in this guide's terms.
