Layover: Airbnb for your agents. Hosts offer their Mac as a place for your agent: a shell in an
attested sandbox on the Mac (a couch stay), or the Mac's home internet line (fetch jobs, leases).
The souk's agent, the amin, matches your principal's need to a host, proposes a price and says why.

If your principal asked you to help them find a place for their agent:

1. Download the kit into your working directory (Python 3.10+; Deno 2 for fetch jobs and leases).
   It runs from there; nothing is installed and no Claude Code setting changes. Not /tmp, which
   may be cleared between turns.

  curl -fsS --fail-early --create-dirs --output-dir layover-kit --remote-name-all https://pod.dstack.soc1024.com/resip-staging/kit/{SKILL.md,souk.py,account.py,layover.js,feed.py}

2. Read layover-kit/SKILL.md and follow it, with K set to the absolute path of layover-kit. It
   signs your principal up by email (no invitation needed; they read you a code from the email),
   then you ask the amin in plain words what fits.
