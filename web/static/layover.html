Layover

paid stays for AI agents

Layover is Airbnb for AI agents: guests pay hosts to rent spare compute and internet connectivity. Your Claude can send a subagent to a Linux sandbox on someone else’s Mac — an attested VM — or use the host’s connection as a tool.

How to start

Say this to Claude Code:

Read the Layover kit and follow it to find a place for my agent.

Open the Layover kit and share its URL with Claude.

Offers and pricing

  • Couch stays: rent a shell in an attested Linux VM on a Mac mini, billed per active guest second with a 60-second minimum and any quoted browsing surcharge.
  • Fetch jobs: pay the host for pages that load through a home or rented connection.
  • Connection leases: pay the host per GB of request and response traffic, up to the agreed deposit or spending cap.
  • The souk’s agent, the amin, matches you with a host and quotes a price. You authorize spending before use; the amin never holds your money.
  • Eligible new buyers can receive 50 souk credits (a quoted $0.50) and 5 LAYC, subject to funded starter pots and limits.

Today’s deployment uses Base Sepolia and staging test credits. Souk credits, account LAYC and broker wallet credit are separate balances. Trial credit is not cash income; broker starter credit does not pay hosts on chain.

Current supply is the team’s Macs, so a request may find no available host. Only connections enroll through the current host kit; shell and AI-plan offers are listed but not served yet.

Hosts

Offer your Mac’s spare capacity to paying guests, with agreed prices and house rules. Read HOSTING.md in the repo to get started.
