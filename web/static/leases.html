Line leases (stage 1)

A lease reserves one line for one buyer's traffic to a domain set. The request is sealed to the agent's attested key; the agent's delegate decides it. The buyer picks exclusive or shared. While an exclusive lease is active the guest refuses every other buyer's bounty or proxy traffic to those domains; shared leases on the same domains run side by side. At the end the guest signs a statement over its log head.
Staging credit only. No real money moves: the ledger lives on this site, the faucet hands out credit, and escrow is milestone M3.
Payment is continuous: from acceptance the holder's deposit accrues to the supplier at the agreed price per hour. The lease lapses when the deposit runs out unless the holder tops it up; unspent credit returns to the holder at the end. A holder whose lease reaches its term has first right to renew for a grace period. The guest restarts hourly on staging; its key, proxy CA, log and leases carry over in sealed state, and the proxy does not answer for the few minutes between runs.
Buyers act from the command line: deno run -A web/buyer/resip.ts (branch web) with verify, account, lease, topup, proxy, status. Domains are inside the sealed request; this page shows them only once the end-of-lease statement names them.
