Your leases

Guests pay hosts at the agreed price per GB of request and response traffic, up to the deposit or authorized cap. A lease ends when its traffic budget or time window runs out; older leases may show hourly billing.
Test payments only: token-backed leases settle in LAYC through GuestEscrow on Base Sepolia; legacy leases use separate staging-credit ledgers. Unused site credit returns when the lease ends; unused token funds become releasable under the escrow's close and claim-grace rules. These balances are not cash host income.
Read your leases with your account secret and optional reply keys. These private records stay in this browser. Use Forget to clear them.
